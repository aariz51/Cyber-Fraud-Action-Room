# Reference passage

Read this out loud at a normal, relaxed pace. It takes about 30 seconds.
Do not perform it. Just talk the way you would explain something to a friend.

Why this text: it is deliberately full of the sounds the narration needs, so the
clone has heard every vowel and consonant it will have to reproduce.

---

> Hello, my name is Aariz. I am a developer based in India, and I build mobile and
> web applications for a living.
>
> This recording is a sample of my natural speaking voice. I am reading it in an
> ordinary room, at an ordinary pace, without trying to sound like a presenter.
>
> Over the past year I have worked on health apps, payment systems, and tools that
> help people deal with government services. The work I enjoy most is the kind that
> removes friction for somebody who is stuck, confused, or short of time.
>
> Numbers I use often include three, seven, twelve, forty, and one hundred. Words
> I use often include design, research, security, and question.
>
> That is the end of the sample. Thank you for listening.
