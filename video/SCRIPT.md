# Golden Hour - voiceover script

8 takes. Read each one as a separate recording. If you fluff one, just redo that
one take. Speak at a normal, unhurried pace: the whole thing lands around 110
seconds, which leaves room under the 2 minute cap.

Pronunciation: **1930** is "nineteen thirty". **CFCFRMS** is spelled out,
"C F C F R M S".

---

### Take 1  (~12s)
> In Indian cyber fraud, the outcome is decided in the first hour. Report within
> minutes and there is a sixty percent chance the money can still be frozen.
> After a week, it is five percent. Nothing in the system today tells you that.

### Take 2  (~13s)
> Golden Hour asks four questions. How much, how it left, when, and the one
> nobody asks. Did you approve the payment? That last answer decides which law
> protects you.

### Take 3  (~16s)
> Now the clock is running. Fifty seven percent, falling one point three points
> every ten minutes. And this is where your money actually is. Still mostly in
> the first account, the only layer banks reliably catch.

### Take 4  (~15s)
> The official flow opens with a form. But the freeze request is what stops
> money, so that leads. Call nineteen thirty first, with a script, in Hindi or
> English, because people go blank on the phone.

### Take 5  (~12s)
> Six hours later. The odds have fallen to forty one percent. The money has
> scattered across forty three accounts. That is the whole argument, made visible.

### Take 6  (~15s)
> Second journey. Your own account frozen because you received one tainted rupee.
> A hundred and seventy five rupees disputed, eighteen lakh held. We test that
> freeze against real law, and draft the letter.

### Take 7  (~18s)
> An OpenAI model does exactly one job here. It turns a panicking person's story
> into the chronology a police officer can act on. Everything else is
> deterministic, because someone deciding what to do in ten minutes deserves the
> same answer every time.

### Take 8  (~13s)
> Every figure carries its source. Every mock is listed. The one thing a prototype
> cannot have is the write into C F C F R M S. That is the only step that moves
> money, and this is the wrapper that should exist around it.
